About InCompute

Formerly the Computing Club at Inspiria Knowledge Campus, Siliguri—now one student-run hub for people, plans, and campus tech events.

Our story & name change

30 Sep decision

On 30 September 2026, the Computing Club formally adopted the name InCompute to reflect a wider student community across BCA, BSc Data Science, AI/ML, and Cyber Security at Inspiria Knowledge Campus.

At the 19 November meeting in Lab 3, the Executive Committee agreed to build the club’s own website so students no longer have to hunt through scattered chats, spreadsheets, and PDFs for schedules, notices, and event sign-ups.

How the club is organised

Meet the team →
Meets monthly

Executive Committee

Chair, Vice-Chair, Secretary, and Treasurer. The Secretary maintains the roster, event calendar sheet, and meeting minutes; the Treasurer reviews any paid service requests.

Working Committees

Three student committees run daily operations: Program (event delivery and hackathons), Publicity (notices, posters, and consent-recorded gallery), and Nominating (club elections).

Year Advisors & Faculty Guides

3rd and 4th Year Advisors connect batches with club activities, while Faculty Advisors and the Head of the School of Computer Science (Observer) review and approve all public content.

Charter commitments

  • At least 3 events every semester and 6 events each academic year, plus 4 yearly observance markers.
  • Every public event is listed on the official Inspiria website and registers through Inspiria’s official facility.
  • Individual student and faculty names appear only on the Team page—never on posters, event cards, or promo banners.
  • Notices, events, and gallery albums go live only after Faculty Advisor or Chair approval, with recorded photo consent.
